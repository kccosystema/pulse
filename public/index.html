📱 Made for your phone

Make any picture pulse to the beat.

PulsePic is a phone app. Add a picture and a song, and it finds the beat and brings your picture to life, ready to share as a link or post as a video. It’s built for phones, so open it on yours to get started.

Scan with your phone’s camera or go to pulsepic.com on your phone Works on iPhone, Android and tablets. Nothing to install.

What PulsePic does

Finds the beat

Analyzes your whole song first (tempo, every beat, the start of each bar), so effects land right on the kick instead of a moment late.

12 effects, 3 presets

Glow, bounce, rings, sparks, echo, RGB split, spectrum bars, strobe and more. Or tap Chill, Club or Hype.

Videos for Reels, Stories & TikTok

Exports a 1080p MP4 in 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5. Pick 15–90 seconds from the drop and post it straight to Instagram.

Share links

Get a link to a page with your pulsing picture, song, artist info and website. Previews look great in texts and socials.

Jam counter

Every share page shows how many times it’s been played, and your links show their counts too.

Studio files welcome

Drop in a WAV, AIFF or FLAC and it’s shrunk on your phone before upload. Song details fill in from the file.

Edit anytime

Change effects, the picture or song details later. Your link stays the same and updates for everyone.

Your files stay yours

Everything happens on your phone until you choose to share. Sign in with Google or Apple to keep your links forever.

Ready? Grab your phone.Scan the code or open pulsepic.com in your phone’s browser.
