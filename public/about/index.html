Why I built PulsePic

In short

I got tired of paying weekly subscription fees for simple tools on my phone, so I started building my own. PulsePic is one of them: I made it to replace BeatLeap. It's free, it runs in your phone's browser, and there's nothing to install.

Tired of weekly app fees

A lot of the apps on my phone had quietly turned into weekly subscriptions. Pay every week just to make a picture move to a song, trim a clip or add an effect. Those little charges add up fast, and most of these tools aren't doing anything a modern phone can't handle on its own.

So I started making my own versions of the tools I actually use. PulsePic is one of them. I built it to replace BeatLeap, the app I was paying for to make my pictures pulse to music.

What PulsePic does

Add a picture and a song. PulsePic finds the beat, brings your picture to life with beat-synced effects, and lets you save a video for Reels, Stories or TikTok or share a link with your song and artist info. Everything happens on your phone until you choose to share.

More features are coming

PulsePic is still growing, and more features are coming soon. If there's something you'd love to see, keep an eye on the blog for what's new.

Will it stay free?

That's the plan. I'll do my best to keep PulsePic free. No weekly fees, no subscriptions. It's 100% free to use… until the hosting bill says otherwise.
