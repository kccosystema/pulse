What "This Has Been Jammed X Times" Means: How PulsePic Counts Plays

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

The jam counter at the top of every PulsePic share page shows how many times the song has been played. A play counts once someone has listened for at least 5 seconds, and each browser counts at most once per day, so quick taps and replays don't inflate the number. You can see the count for each of your links in My links.

Where do I see the jam count?

  • On the share page: at the top it says "🔥 This has been jammed X times!" Before the first play it says "Be the first to jam this!"
  • In My links: when you're signed in, each of your links shows its jam count, so you can see which songs are getting the most love.

What counts as a jam?

A jam is a real listen, not just a page view. It counts when:

  • the listener has heard at least 5 seconds of the song, and
  • that browser hasn't already counted a jam for this song today.

So if someone plays your song ten times in an afternoon, it counts once that day. If they come back tomorrow and play it again, that's another jam. This keeps the number honest and hard to inflate by refreshing.

Why 5 seconds?

It filters out accidental taps and people who bounce straight away, while still counting anyone who actually gave the song a listen.

How to get more jams

  • Post an exported video to Reels, Stories or TikTok and put the share link in your bio or caption.
  • Send the link directly: its preview shows your picture, which gets more taps than a plain link.
  • Keep it on the homepage: leave Show on the PulsePic homepage ticked so new visitors can find it in "Fresh on PulsePic".
  • Freshen it up: edit the picture or effects without losing your count or changing the link.

Is the count private?

The number is public on the share page, so it doubles as social proof. PulsePic counts plays without identifying who played them.

Frequently asked questions

Does refreshing the page add a jam?

No. Each browser counts at most one jam per song per day, and only after 5 seconds of listening.

Does editing my link reset the jam count?

No. Editing the picture, effects or details keeps the same link and the same count.

Do my own plays count?

Yes, like anyone else's: once per day per browser, after 5 seconds.

What happens to the count if I delete the link?

Deleting a link removes the page, its files and its play count.
