Effects and presets

12 Beat-Synced Effects for Your Cover Art (and 3 One-Tap Presets)

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

PulsePic has 12 effects that fire on the beat: Glow, Shake, Flash, Tilt, Bounce, Echo, Rings, Sparks, RGB split, Bars, Strobe and Circle. Turn on as many as you like, or tap a preset: Chill (soft glow, rings and echo), Club (glow, flash, rings, bars and tilt) or Hype (almost everything at full strength).

Every effect, explained

Tap Effects in the player to open the grid, then tap any effect to switch it on or off. They all follow the beat you chose under Sync to.

Glow
A colored halo around your picture that flares on each beat and shifts color as the song goes.
Shake
A quick jolt on the beat. Short and punchy, great for heavy drops.
Flash
A bright flash over the picture on the beat.
Tilt
The picture rocks left and right, alternating each beat.
Bounce
The picture hops up on the beat and settles back down.
Echo
Fading copies of the picture spread out behind it, like a visual delay.
Rings
Outlines ripple out from the edges of the picture on each beat.
Sparks
Bursts of colored particles, strongest on the first beat of each bar.
RGB split
The red, green and blue layers pull apart on the beat for a glitchy, retro look.
Bars
Spectrum bars around the picture that dance with the actual frequencies of the song, bass to treble.
Strobe
A hard strobe on the first beat of each bar. Use it sparingly.
Circle
Crops the picture into a circle, like a spinning record. Works with all the other effects.

What do the presets do?

Presets are one-tap starting points. They set the effects and the Strength for you, and you can change anything afterwards.

  • Chill: Glow, Rings and Echo at low strength. Smooth and laid-back, good for acoustic, lo-fi and R&B.
  • Club: Glow, Flash, Rings, Bars and Tilt at medium strength. Good for dance, house and pop.
  • Hype: Glow, Shake, Flash, RGB split, Sparks, Strobe, Echo and Bounce at high strength. Made for drops, trap and hard EDM.

How do I make the effects stronger or softer?

Use the Strength slider. It scales every effect at once, from a gentle breathing pulse to a full-on light show. If the beat feels too busy, switch Sync to Beat; if it's not busy enough, try All hits.

Tips for great-looking results

  • Less is often more. Two or three effects usually look more polished than all twelve.
  • Match the song's energy. Chill for verses and ballads, Hype for the drop.
  • Use Circle for album art. It gives a vinyl or "now playing" feel that reads well on social feeds.
  • Tap Hide to tuck the controls away and see the picture full screen.
  • Be kind to eyes. If you or your viewers are sensitive to flashing light, leave Flash and Strobe off.

Frequently asked questions

How many effects can I use at once?

As many as you like. All 12 can run together, and the Strength slider controls how intense they are.

Do my effect choices carry over to the share link and video?

Yes. The share page plays your picture with the same effects, strength and sync settings, and exported videos include them too.

Which preset is best for Instagram Reels?

Club is a safe all-rounder. Use Hype for high-energy drops and Chill for slower songs.

Can I change effects after I share a link?

Yes, if you are signed in. Open My links, tap Edit, change the effects and save. The link stays the same.
