Editing

How to Edit a PulsePic Share Link Without Changing the URL

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

Sign in, tap your account button, open My links and tap Edit on the song. PulsePic opens it in the player, where you can change the effects, strength, sync mode, song details and picture. Tap Save changes and the same link updates for everyone, usually within a minute. Your jam count is kept.

What can I change?

  • Effects and presets, Strength, Sensitivity and the Sync to mode.
  • Song details: song name, artist, album and website.
  • The picture: tap Change picture in the save screen to swap the artwork.
  • Homepage visibility: show or hide the song in "Fresh on PulsePic".

The song itself can't be swapped on an existing link, since the link and its jam count belong to that song. For a new version or remix, create a new link.

How to edit a link, step by step

  1. Open pulsepic.com on your phone and sign in (top right).
  2. Tap your account button to open My links.
  3. Tap Edit on the song you want to change. It opens in the player with its current settings.
  4. Make your changes: effects, details or picture.
  5. Tap Share, then Save changes.

Anyone opening the link will see the new version. It can take up to a minute to show up everywhere.

Why keep the same link?

Your link is probably already in your bio, in old posts and in people's messages. Editing in place means all of those keep working, and your jam count keeps growing instead of starting over.

Do I need an account to edit?

Yes. Editing is tied to your account so that only you can change your links. If you made a link before signing in, sign in on the same phone within 30 days and it moves into your account, ready to edit. Here's how accounts work.

Other things you can do in My links

  • Copy a link to paste anywhere.
  • Open the share page to check how it looks.
  • Delete a link to remove the page, picture, song and play count.

Frequently asked questions

Does editing change my share link?

No. The URL stays exactly the same; only what the page shows changes.

How long until my edits show up?

Usually right away, and within about a minute everywhere.

Can I replace the song on a link?

No. Each link belongs to one song. Create a new link for a new version.

Will editing reset my jam count?

No. The count is kept when you edit.
