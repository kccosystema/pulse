Video export

How to Turn a Song and a Picture Into a Video for Instagram Reels, Stories and TikTok

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

Open PulsePic on your phone, add a picture and a song, pick your effects, then tap 🎬 Video. Choose Story / Reel (9:16), Square (1:1) or Feed (4:5), pick 15, 30, 60 or 90 seconds or the full song, and tap Record video. PulsePic makes a 1080p MP4 you can save to your phone and post to Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else.

Step by step

  1. Go to pulsepic.com on your phone. It runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install. Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android work best.
  2. Pick a picture and a song. Cover art, a photo or a flyer, and your track (MP3, M4A, WAV and more).
  3. Set the look. Choose a preset or your own effects, and add your song details so the song name and artist show on the video.
  4. Choose where the clip starts. Drag the song slider to the part you want, like the chorus or the drop. The video starts from there.
  5. Tap 🎬 Video, pick the format and length, and tap Record video.
  6. Save it. Tap Share / Save video and choose Save Video (it goes to your Photos), or share it straight to an app.

Which format should I pick?

  • Story / Reel (9:16, 1080×1920): full-screen vertical video for Instagram Reels and Stories, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  • Square (1:1, 1080×1080): classic feed post, works almost everywhere.
  • Feed (4:5, 1080×1350): the tallest size Instagram shows in the main feed, so it takes up more of the screen.

How long can the video be?

Pick 15, 30, 60 or 90 seconds, or Full for the rest of the song from where the slider is. Shorter clips are quicker to make and tend to do better on Reels and TikTok, so 15–30 seconds of the best part of the song is a great default.

Do I have to wait while it records?

On most recent phones, no. PulsePic builds the video faster than it plays, so a 30-second clip often takes just a few seconds. On phones that can't do that, it falls back to recording the clip as it plays, so a 30-second video takes about 30 seconds. Either way, keep PulsePic open until it finishes.

Will Instagram accept the file?

Yes. On Safari (iPhone) and Chrome (Android), PulsePic saves a standard MP4 (H.264 video with AAC audio), the format Instagram and TikTok expect. Some desktop browsers can only make WebM files, which Instagram doesn't accept; PulsePic warns you if that happens. It's built for phones anyway.

Can I remove the watermark?

Yes. A small "Made with PulsePic.com" credit is on by default. Untick it in the export screen if you'd rather not include it. (We'd love it if you kept it.)

Tips for videos that get watched

  • Start on the hook: drag the slider to the chorus or drop before exporting.
  • Turn on Show song name and artist on the video in Song details so people know what they're hearing.
  • Add your link in the caption or bio: a PulsePic share link lets people hear the song and visit your website.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video export free?

Yes. Exporting videos is free and you do not need an account.

Is my song uploaded to make the video?

No. The video is made on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you separately create a share link.

What resolution are the videos?

1080p: 1080×1920 for Story / Reel, 1080×1080 for Square and 1080×1350 for Feed.

Can I export only the chorus?

Yes. Drag the song slider to where the chorus starts, then export. The video starts from the slider position.

Does it work for TikTok and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Use the Story / Reel (9:16) format; it is the vertical size TikTok and Shorts use.
