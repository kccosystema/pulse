Privacy and accounts

Do My Files Stay Private? How PulsePic Handles Your Picture, Song and Account

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

Your picture and song stay on your phone while you use PulsePic: the beat analysis, effects and video export all happen on the device. Files are only uploaded when you tap Create link to make a share page. You don't need an account to use PulsePic; signing in with Google or Apple just keeps your share links forever instead of 30 days.

What stays on my phone?

  • Beat analysis: PulsePic studies your song on your phone.
  • Effects and preview: everything you see in the player is drawn on your phone.
  • Video export: the MP4 is made on your phone and saved to it. Nothing is uploaded.

When is something uploaded?

Only when you create a share link. Then PulsePic uploads a copy of the picture (as a JPEG, up to 1600 px) and the song (compressed to MP3 if it's a studio file), plus the song details and effect settings you chose. Anyone with the link can see and hear them, so only share music you own or have permission to share.

Do I need an account?

No. You can make pulsing pictures, export videos and create share links without signing in. An account adds:

  • Links that never expire (guest links are deleted after 30 days).
  • My links: every link you've made, with jam counts, in one place.
  • Editing links without changing the URL.

Sign-in uses Google or Apple, so there's no new password to remember. Links you made as a guest on the same phone move into your account when you sign in.

How do I delete my stuff?

Open My links and tap Delete on any link. That removes the share page, the uploaded picture and song, and the play count. Guest links clean themselves up after 30 days.

Does PulsePic track me?

PulsePic keeps simple counts, like how many songs are shared, videos exported and jams played, to see what's working. Jams are counted without identifying who played them.

Is PulsePic really free?

Yes: 100% free to use… until the hosting bill says otherwise. There's no app to buy and no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Is my song uploaded when I make a video?

No. Videos are made and saved on your phone. Uploads only happen when you create a share link.

Do I need an account to use PulsePic?

No. An account is optional; it keeps your share links forever and lets you edit them.

How do I sign in?

Tap Sign in at the top right and choose Google or Apple.

What happens to links I made before signing in?

Sign in on the same phone within 30 days and they move into your account and stop expiring.

Is PulsePic free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no subscription and nothing to install.
