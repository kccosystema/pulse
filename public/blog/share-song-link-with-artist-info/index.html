Share links

How to Share Your Song as a Link With a Pulsing Picture and Artist Info

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

In the PulsePic player, tap Share, confirm you own the music or have permission, and tap Create link. You get a pulsepic.com link to a page that plays your song with your pulsing picture and effects, and shows the song name, artist, album and a button to your website. Links without an account last 30 days; sign in with Google or Apple to keep them forever.

What does the share page show?

  • Your picture, pulsing to the song with the exact effects and settings you chose.
  • The song, ready to play with one tap.
  • Song name, artist, and album if you added one.
  • A button to the artist's website, so listeners can find more music, merch or shows.
  • A jam counter showing how many times it's been played.

When you paste the link into a text, DM or post, it shows a preview with your picture, so it stands out in the conversation.

How to create a share link

  1. Add your picture and song on pulsepic.com and set your effects.
  2. Tap Details and fill in the song name and artist (album and website are optional).
  3. Tap Share, tick I own this music or have permission to share it, and tap Create link.
  4. Copy the link, or tap Share… to send it straight to any app.

How long do share links last?

Without an account, a link lasts 30 days. After that, the page, picture and song are deleted automatically. Sign in with Google or Apple and your links never expire. If you made links before signing in, sign in on the same phone within the 30 days and they move into your account.

Will my song show up on the PulsePic homepage?

By default, new links can appear in the "Fresh on PulsePic" row on the homepage, which helps new listeners find your music. Don't want that? Untick Show on the PulsePic homepage when you create the link, or change it later when you edit the link. Only the picture, song name and artist are shown there.

Can I share music I didn't make?

Only share songs you own or have permission to share. Share links are perfect for artists, producers, DJs and labels promoting their own releases. If you just want to make a video of a song for yourself, use video export instead, which keeps everything on your phone.

Who can see my link?

Anyone who has the link can open it. It doesn't need an account or an app to play. You can delete a link at any time from My links, which removes the page, the picture, the song and its play count.

Frequently asked questions

Do listeners need an account or an app?

No. Share pages open in any browser on any device and play with one tap.

How long does a share link last?

30 days without an account. Links you make while signed in with Google or Apple never expire.

Can I add my website to the share page?

Yes. Add it under Song details (Artist website) and the page shows a button to it.

Can I change the link after sharing it?

Yes, if you are signed in: edit the effects, picture or details and the same link updates for everyone.

How big can the song file be?

Up to 40 MB after PulsePic compresses it. Studio files like WAV are converted to MP3 on your phone first, so most songs fit easily.
