Can I Use a WAV File? Uploading Studio Masters to PulsePic

By the PulsePic Team · November 11, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

Yes. PulsePic works with WAV, AIFF and FLAC files as well as MP3, M4A, AAC and OGG. The beat is found from your original file. If you create a share link, PulsePic converts studio files to a high-quality MP3 on your phone first, so the upload is small and the share page loads fast. The original file never leaves your phone.

Which audio files work?

Any format your phone's browser can play, including MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC and OGG. That covers bounces straight out of most DAWs and files sent by your engineer or producer.

What happens to a WAV file when I share it?

A 4-minute WAV is around 40 MB, which is slow to upload and slow for listeners to load. So when you create a share link, PulsePic converts WAV, AIFF and FLAC files to MP3 on your phone, with a progress bar while it works:

  • 192 kbps for stereo songs, 128 kbps for mono.
  • The beat timing is kept exactly in line with the original, so the pulse stays on the beat.
  • Any other file over 40 MB is compressed the same way.

The finished upload can be up to 40 MB, which fits songs well over half an hour long at these settings.

Does compressing change the video quality?

No. Video export uses the song you picked, straight from your phone, and encodes the audio for the video itself. Compression only applies to the copy uploaded for a share link.

Will PulsePic read my song's title and artist?

For MP3 files with tags, yes: PulsePic fills in the song name, artist and album from the file automatically. WAV files usually don't carry tags, so just type the details in under Song details.

What about my picture?

Use any image your phone can open: cover art, photos, flyers or screenshots. For share links, PulsePic saves a copy as a JPEG up to 1600 pixels on the long side, which keeps the page fast while still looking sharp on any phone.

Tips for the best result

  • Use your final master, not a rough mix: the beat detection loves a clear, punchy low end.
  • Square cover art (like 3000×3000) looks best, especially with the Circle effect.
  • On a slow connection, stay on the page until the upload finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Is my original WAV file uploaded?

No. Only the compressed MP3 copy is uploaded, and only if you create a share link. The original stays on your phone.

What bitrate does PulsePic use?

192 kbps MP3 for stereo files and 128 kbps for mono.

Can I upload a FLAC or AIFF file?

Yes. FLAC and AIFF are converted to MP3 on your phone before uploading, just like WAV.

What is the maximum song size?

40 MB after compression, which at 192 kbps is roughly 29 minutes of stereo audio.
